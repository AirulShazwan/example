GOOD TO KNOW

A smoother trip
starts here.

Just a few things to keep in mind before you head north.

01

Getting around

Driving or arranging a car is often the most practical way to visit attractions spread across the state. Check transport options ahead of time.

02

Plan for the weather

Outdoor views depend on weather and visibility. Bring water, sun protection and a flexible backup plan for rainy days.

03

Respect local culture

Dress considerately when visiting religious sites, support small local businesses and leave natural spaces as you found them.
